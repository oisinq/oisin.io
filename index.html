Hi! I'm Oisín Quinn. I’m a software engineer focused on building front-ends on the web. I live in Dublin, Ireland but I'm originally from the lovely Cavan.

I currently work for HubSpot, where I build products using React and TypeScript. I consider myself to be a product engineer, and I care much more about building the right thing than using fancy new tech.

I've tried my hand at a lot of different tech over the past few years, but I'm a big fan of JavaScript and front-end software development (especially React). I grew up as a kid always on the internet, writing blog posts about all the latest Club Penguin tips and tricks and tweeting at way too young of an age. I was amazed at how easy it was to create something as complex as a website, and easily build something that people could start using straight-away.

I love building things on the web, but I also love music. I love listening to it, talking about it, going to gigs, and following my local scene in Dublin.

.

What I’ve been up to:

  • I hosted a college radio show called Quinntessentials for three years. This show aired weekly on Belfield FM and I uploaded most of these episodes to Mixcloud. Some of these have been copyright-striked since then, but I have an archive of all episodes here.
  • I co-organised a student-run tech conference called SISTEM for two years. 2020’s edition featured 23 different events, 30+ speakers and had 200 attendees, raising over €1,500 for charity. It was the most challenging and stressful thing I’ve ever done in my life, but also probably the most rewarding! Learn more about it here.
  • amhran.ie was a React website that showcased a new Irish song every day. I learned a lot about the Irish music scene while running this project. In hindsight I bit off way more than I could chew with this (finding a new good Irish song every day is tough!), but it was cool to just come up with an idea and launch it within a week. Learn more on my blog here.

...and that’s about it! I’ve recently had a lot of ideas for things I want to do, but I’ve struggled to actually put those ideas into action. Maybe that’ll change soon?
